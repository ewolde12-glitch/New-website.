Lion Information

The lion is one of the greatest leaders among wild animals. Known as the king of the jungle, the lion is strong, brave, and powerful. Lions live in groups called prides and work together to hunt, protect their territory, and care for their young. The lion is a symbol of courage, strength, and leadership.
